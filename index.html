ABOUT

Background, technical positioning, and practical offensive security focus

I am a cybersecurity enthusiast focused on offensive security, specializing in Web Application Penetration Testing and Network Penetration Testing. I have hands-on experience with security labs, bug hunting, and practical platforms such as TryHackMe and PortSwigger Web Security Academy.

My approach to cybersecurity is centered around thinking like an attacker to identify vulnerabilities before they can be exploited. I aim to help organizations discover and address security weaknesses that could allow attackers to gain unauthorized access to sensitive systems or information, potentially leading to data breaches, financial losses, and serious operational impact.

By identifying vulnerabilities and understanding how real-world attacks work, I help organizations reduce their security risks, protect valuable data, and minimize the potential losses caused by successful attacks. I am continuously developing my skills by exploring different attack scenarios, penetration testing methodologies, and security techniques to better understand both how systems can be compromised and how they can be strengthened against these threats.
